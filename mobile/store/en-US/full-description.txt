PropertyQuarry turns a scattered property search into one calm, private decision workspace.

Bring listings together from supported property sites, compare the facts that matter, and keep every serious candidate in a visual shortlist. PropertyQuarry helps you understand the home, the location and the missing information before you decide what deserves your time.

Designed for thoughtful property decisions:

• Share a listing directly from another Android app and confirm before it is added
• Review a premium, image-led shortlist with diorama previews where available
• Compare price, space, location evidence, soft filters and open questions
• Watch a normal camera walkthrough as the default property video
• Open a 3DVista or Matterport experience when an immersive 3D tour is available
• Choose VR or 3D-glasses viewing only when you want it and the tour supports it
• Keep research, evidence and decisions tied to the correct property

Your PropertyQuarry account remains the authority. Sign-in happens in your trusted browser and returns through a short-lived, one-time secure handoff. Shared links are validated by the PropertyQuarry service before they enter your private shortlist.

An internet connection is required for live property data, research and tours. When the service cannot be verified, the app stays on a clear retry screen instead of loading an untrusted or stale workspace.
